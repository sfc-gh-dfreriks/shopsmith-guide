# Table saw

Use it for crosscuts, rips and miters. [520 Summary p.24–25]

## Set up

- [ ] Set the dial to **Slow**, switch off and **unplug**. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] Position the headstock so you can reach the spindle, then **lock the quill**. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] **Mount the lower guard** on the quill. The stop screw butts the quill stop collar, and the ridge on the headstock lines up with the slot in the collar. Tighten the collar screw. Loosen the two knobs and slide the guard cover **to the right**. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] **Put the blade on the arbor** with the teeth pointing toward you. *The arbor nut loosens **clockwise**.* Finger-tighten it, then snug it with the arbor wrench while you hold the arbor with an adjustable wrench. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] **Mount the arbor** on the spindle and tighten the setscrew on the flat. Slide the guard cover back in toward the blade **before** you lower the table, or you'll damage the guard. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] Raise the table fully. Slide the carriage in until it touches the rubber spacer. Lower the table until the blade is **1/4" above the stock thickness**, centered in the insert. Lock the height and carriage. [520 Summary p.21]
- [ ] Slide the upper guard's **riving knife** through the insert into the lower guard and tighten the guard lock knob. The knife sits about **1/8"** from the blade, centered on it. [520 Summary p.18, p.21]
- [ ] Do the **five-point lock check**. [520 Summary p.21]

**Speed:** general sawing **R (3500)**. Heavy ripping **O (2800)** for hardwood, **P (3000)** for softwood. Trim cuts **S (3800)**. [520 Summary p.24]

## First cut: a square crosscut

- [ ] Put the miter gauge in the slot on the side you're standing, set to 90°. [520 Summary p.24]
- [ ] Mark the cut so the blade runs on the **waste side** of the line. Set the safety grip's quick clamp to the stock thickness. [520 Summary p.24]
- [ ] Squeeze the safety grip. Switch on at Slow, **then** set **R**, and feed steadily. [520 Summary p.24]
- [ ] Return the dial to **Slow**, switch off, and wait for the blade to **stop** before you reach for the pieces. [520 Summary p.24]

## First rip

- [ ] Lock the rip fence at the width you want. Use the quill feed to fine-tune the distance. [520 Summary p.25]
- [ ] Mount a **feather board in front of the blade**, never beside or behind it. [520 Summary p.25]
- [ ] Keep the stock against the fence. Finish with a **push stick** (1-1/2"–5" stock) or **push block** (over 5"). [520 Summary p.3, p.25]

## Common mistakes

- Turning the speed dial with the machine stopped. [520 Summary p.6]
- Lowering the table before sliding the guard cover in. [520 Summary p.21]
- Putting the feather board behind the blade, which causes kickback. [520 Summary p.25]
- Ripping with a fence that isn't parallel, or with a crooked edge against the fence. Never reach over the blade. [520 Summary p.25]
- Forcing the feed. If the saw bogs down, slow down. [520 Summary p.24]
