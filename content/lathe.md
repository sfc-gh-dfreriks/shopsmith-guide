# Lathe

Use it for spindle turning between centers and for faceplate turning. Swing is **16-1/2"**, and there's **34"** between centers. [520 Summary p.8, p.30]

## Set up

- [ ] Set the dial to **Slow**, switch off and **unplug**. [520 Summary p.23]
- [ ] **Remove the worktable.** Tilt it 90° left, lock the tilt, loosen the height lock, and lift it straight up. [520 Summary p.23]
- [ ] Mount the **tool rest** in the carriage so its rack meshes with the pinion. [520 Summary p.23]
- [ ] Mount the **drive center** fully on the spindle, setscrew on the flat. [520 Summary p.23]
- [ ] Mount the **tailstock** in the right **base mount**, with the cup center pointing at the headstock. [520 Summary p.23]
- [ ] Tighten the accessory mount lock. Check the carriage, table height, headstock and quill locks. This is a four-point check, because the table is off. [520 Summary p.20, p.23]

*A stuck cup center taps out from behind with a nylon or rawhide mallet, never a steel hammer.* [520 Summary p.23]

**Speed:** big, heavy stock needs slower speeds. [520 Summary p.30]

| Stock diameter | Rounding | Shaping | Sanding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 2" | C (950) | F (1300) | K (2050) |
| 2"–4" | B (850) | E (1150) | J (1900) |
| 4"–6" | A (750) | D (1050) | H (1600) |
| Over 6" | Slow | A (750) | B (850) |

## First job: turn a cylinder

- [ ] Use sound stock with no splits or loose knots. [Lathe Turning p.7]
  - For glue-ups, long pieces or anything over 3" in diameter, check the balance: hang it on nails at the centers and remove at most 1/32" at a time from the heavy side. [520 Summary p.30]
- [ ] Seat the drive center in the end with a plastic or rawhide mallet. Mount the stock and press the quill feed so the spurs and the cup bite. [Lathe Turning p.8–9]
- [ ] Set the tool rest **no more than 1/4"** from the stock. **Spin the stock by hand** to check that it clears. [Lathe Turning p.8–9]
- [ ] Start at **Slow**, then set the **rounding** speed. Rough the stock to a cylinder with a gouge. [Lathe Turning p.10; 520 Summary p.30]
- [ ] Size it with a parting tool, then shape it at the **shaping** speed. [Lathe Turning p.10]
- [ ] **Remove the tool rest** before sanding, and sand at the **sanding** speed. [Lathe Turning p.12]

**Two ways to cut:** [520 Summary p.30]
- **Scraping:** the rest sits just below center, with the chisel pointing at the center of the stock.
- **Shearing:** the rest sits 1/4"–1/2" below the top of the stock, with the chisel held at an angle.

## Common mistakes

- Forcing or jabbing the tool. [Lathe Turning p.2, p.8]
- Running large stock too fast. [520 Summary p.30]
- Touching the spinning stock while the tool rest is mounted. [Lathe Turning p.8]
- Wrapping sandpaper all the way around the spindle. [Lathe Turning p.12]
- Parting a piece fully off on the lathe. [Lathe Turning p.8, p.12]
- Shear-cutting faceplate work whose grain runs across the axis. [Lathe Turning p.14]
