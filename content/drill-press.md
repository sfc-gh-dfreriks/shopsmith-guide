# Drill press

On the Mark V, the **whole way-tube assembly pivots up on the base**; the headstock doesn't tilt on its own. [520 Summary p.6, p.22]

## Raise it to vertical

- [ ] Set the dial to **Slow**, switch off and **unplug**. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] Put the headstock **10"** from the headrest (right end) and the carriage **20"** from the base mount. Tighten both locks, without overtightening. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] Raise the table surface just above the top of the headstock and lock the height. **Tilt the table 90° left** and lock the tilt. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] Mount the **drill chuck** fully on the spindle, setscrew on the flat. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] **Raise any casters.** [520 Summary p.10]
- [ ] Loosen the **headrest lock**. With one hand on each side of the headstock, lift the machine to vertical. Tighten the **base lock**. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] Rock the tubes side to side and retighten if you can. The base lock sits off-center in its hole on purpose. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] *(Optional)* Move the quill feed handle to the back. [520 Summary p.22]
- [ ] Chuck the bit and **remove the key**. Do the lock check. [520 Summary p.22]

> **Never move the headstock while the machine is vertical.** It can drop. [520 Summary p.22]

**Speed:** for brad-point and twist bits. For other bits, follow the bit maker's recommendation. [520 Summary p.27]

| Hole size | Hardwood | Softwood |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 1/4" | H (1600) | I (1750) |
| 1/4"–1/2" | F (1300) | G (1450) |
| 1/2"–3/4" | D (1050) | E (1150) |
| 3/4"–1" | B (850) | C (950) |
| Over 1" | Slow (700) | A (750) |
| Metal (twist bits only) | Slow (700) | Slow (700) |

## First job: drill a through hole

- [ ] Use the rip fence (or the miter gauge) as a backstop. **Clamp a wider scrap board under the stock.** [520 Summary p.27–28; Drill Press p.3]
- [ ] **Hold the carriage so it can't drop**, then loosen it. Set the bit tip **1/4"–1/2"** above the stock and relock. [520 Summary p.27]
- [ ] With the stock out of the way, extend the quill until the flutes touch the scrap. Set the **depth control to 1/8"** and lock it. [520 Summary p.28]
- [ ] Check the hole position with the machine off. [520 Summary p.28]
- [ ] Switch on, set the speed, and feed slowly. Back out to clear chips. [520 Summary p.28]
- [ ] When the stop hits, retract. Return to **Slow** and switch off. [520 Summary p.28]

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the chuck key in, or wearing gloves. [Drill Press p.10]
- Not locking the table tilt and tubes, or not clamping the work. [Drill Press p.3, p.10]
- Stopping the chuck with your hand. [Drill Press p.10]
- Chucking tapered square-shank bits, or using a screw-lead auger with no pilot hole. [Drill Press p.10]
- Working with the table far from mid-chest height. [Drill Press p.10]
