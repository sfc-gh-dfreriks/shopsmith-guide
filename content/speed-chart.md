# Speed chart

> **Dial rules:** start at **Slow**, turn the dial **only while running**, and return to **Slow** before switching off. [520 Summary p.3, p.6]

## Your five modes (520 Summary Manual)

| Operation | Hardwood | Softwood |
|---|---|---|
| **Saw**: general sawing | R (3500) | R (3500) |
| **Saw**: heavy ripping | O (2800) | P (3000) |
| **Saw**: trim cuts | S (3800) | S (3800) |
| **Disc sander**: coarse 60# | D (1050) | E (1150) |
| **Disc sander**: medium 80# | F (1300) | G (1450) |
| **Disc sander**: fine 100# | G (1450) | H (1600) |
| **Disc sander**: grinding or sharpening metal | Slow (700) | Slow (700) |
| **Drill and bore**: up to 1/4" | H (1600) | I (1750) |
| **Drill and bore**: 1/4"–1/2" | F (1300) | G (1450) |

| **Drill and bore**: metal (twist bits) | Slow (700) | Slow (700) |

Sources: [520 Summary p.24, p.26, p.27, p.29]. Go a little slower for end grain on the disc sander, because it burns easily. [520 Summary p.26]
