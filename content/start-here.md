# Start here: your machine & safety

## 1. Confirm which Mark V you have

- [ ] **Find the model number** on the vent plate around the auxiliary spindle (at the back of the headstock). [Lathe Duplicator manual p.2]
- [ ] **Check the fence and table.** A **Model 520** has a **Pro Fence** with an **upper and a lower lock handle**, and the tables ride on **front and rear rail extrusions**. [520 Summary p.17; 510→520 Upgrade p.1]
  - A **510** instead clamps its rip fence onto round **table tubes**. [Alignment & Maintenance p.7–8]
- [ ] **Check the speed control.** A **520** has a mechanical speed dial (the motor drives a variable-diameter pulley). A **520S** has an electronic variable-speed (PowerPro) motor. [520 Summary p.8; 520S Owner's Manual p.22]

> **A 2003 machine with Pro Fence rails is consistent with a Model 520.** The 520 upgrade literature is dated 9/01. Your manuals don't include a model-by-year table or a serial-number decoder, so trust the vent plate. [510→520 Upgrade p.4]
>
> This guide is written for the **Model 520**. If yours turns out to be a 520S, follow the 520S notes where they differ.

## 2. The Shopsmith rules (read before first power-up)

1. **Turn the speed dial only while the machine is running.** Turning it while stopped damages the speed changer. [520 Summary p.6]
2. **Always start at "Slow".** This avoids blowing fuses and tripping breakers. Return the dial to **Slow** after every operation, then switch off. [520 Summary p.3, p.6] *(The 520S manual says "D" instead.)* [520S Owner's Manual p.85]
3. **Set the working speed after switch-on but before you cut.** A sanding disc at saw speed can blow fuses. A lathe at router speed can throw the stock. [520 Summary p.20–21]
4. **Switch off and unplug** before any adjustment, mode change or maintenance. [520 Summary p.4]
5. **Do the five-point lock check** before every start. Sweep your hand clockwise over the locks:
   1. table tilt
   2. carriage
   3. table height
   4. headstock
   5. quill

   Leave the quill lock **loose** for drilling, boring and quill-fed sanding. [520 Summary p.20]
6. **To tighten a lock**, spin it until it stops, then add **1/4 turn**. Overtightening damages the tubes. The carriage lock handle must end up **horizontal**. [520 Summary p.10, p.20]
7. **To mount an accessory**, push it fully onto the spindle and tighten its setscrew on the spindle **flat** with the 5/32" Allen wrench. Rock it while tightening to remove play. [520 Summary p.20]
8. **Keep the saw guards on.** Only for non-through cuts (dado, rabbet, molding) do you remove the **upper** guard, and the lower guard always stays on. [520 Summary p.2]
9. **Never stand in line with a blade, cutter or disc**, and keep your hands at least **3"** away. [520 Summary p.4]
10. **Never let go of the quill feed lever** while the quill is extended unless the quill lock is set. [520 Summary p.5]
11. **Remove the chuck key** every time. [520 Summary p.22–23]
12. **Never move the headstock while the machine is vertical.** [520 Summary p.22]
13. **In use, the machine sits on the floor, not on its casters.** [520 Summary p.5]
14. **With two tools mounted, never exceed the speed of the slowest one.** [520 Summary p.4]

## 3. Electrical

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Circuit | 15 A minimum. The motor can draw 13–14 A under heavy load. [520 Summary p.3] |
| Fuse or breaker | Time-delay, no larger than 15 A (Fusetron T-15 recommended). [520 Summary p.3] |
| Extension cord | 25 ft = 14 AWG, 50 ft = 12 AWG, 100 ft = 10 AWG. [520 Summary p.3] |

## 4. First power-up checklist

- [ ] The machine is on a level floor with the casters retracted. [520 Summary p.5, p.9]
- [ ] Nothing is mounted on any spindle, the spindle caps are on, and the guards are clear.
- [ ] The five locks are tight (quill locked).
- [ ] The switch is **off** before plugging in. [520 Summary p.4]
- [ ] Insert the safety key, switch on, and listen at **Slow**.
- [ ] With it running, slowly turn the dial up through the letters and back to **Slow**.
- [ ] Switch off and wait for a full stop.
- [ ] Read the **Alignment & maintenance** page before your first cut. Machines that have been moved often need it. [520 Summary p.9]

## 5. Safety kit sizing

The **push stick** is for stock 1-1/2"–5" wide, the **push block** for stock over 5", and the **fence straddler** for stock under 1-1/2". [520 Summary p.3]
